Premium and purchases

What does Premium add?

Groups, your own prayers, making any prayer a devotion, Stacks, and as many devotions as you like. Prayers, the Bible, readings and streaks are always free.

How do I cancel?

In your phone's Settings under Subscriptions (iPhone) or in the Play Store (Android). Premium stays on until the time you paid for runs out.

Can I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle payments, so ask them for a refund: on iPhone at reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android through your Play Store order history.

How do I get Premium on a new phone?

Sign in with the same account, open the Premium page and tap Restore purchases.

What happens to my groups if Premium ends?

You're paused, so you don't break the group's streak. When you renew, you can catch up or jump back in.

Streaks and devotions

When does a new day start?

At 3 a.m., so late-night prayers count for that day.

How does my streak work?

It counts the days in a row you mark at least one devotion done.

I missed a day. Can I catch up?

Yes. Open the devotion and pray the days you missed. Novenas can be caught up but not prayed ahead.

Can I get a lost streak back?

If your streak was over 30 days and you missed 1 or 2 days, Today offers "Win back your streak." You can do this once a month.

How do I change reminders?

Open the devotion and change its reminder time under Details. You can turn all reminders off in Settings.

Groups

Who can make or join a group?

Anyone with Premium.

How do I join a group?

Ask a member for the invite code, then go to Groups → Join with a code. If the group is partway through a devotion, you start at Day 1 and can catch up.

How does a group streak work?

The group keeps its streak when every member has prayed or is caught up. Paused and offline members don't break it.

What if I'll have no signal?

Turn on Going offline in Group info. The streak waits for you.

What are nudges and emergency requests?

A nudge reminds someone to pray. An emergency request asks the whole group to pray now, up to 3 a day per group.

How do I block someone?

Press and hold their message in the group chat and tap Block.

How do I leave a group?

Open Group info and tap Leave group.

Using the app offline

Does the app work without signal?

Yes. Your Today devotions are saved on your phone. Mark them done and they'll sync later.

Can I read the whole Bible offline?

Yes, after you download a Bible in Settings.

What doesn't work offline?

Searching for new devotions, Suggest, group chat and signing out.

Your account

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Account → Delete account. It erases everything and can't be undone.

Does deleting my account cancel Premium?

No. Cancel it in your phone's Settings first.

What do you do with my data?

See our Privacy Policy.

Traditions and prayers

Is Pocket Chapel only for Catholics?

No, it's for all Christians. Change what the Library shows in Settings.

Where do the prayers come from?

Books published in 1930 or earlier. The Readings of the Day show the Bible verses for each day in your own Bible translation.

I found a mistake in a prayer.

Email and tell us which prayer. We'll fix it.
