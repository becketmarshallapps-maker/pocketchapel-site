Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Pocket Chapel ("we", "us") is a prayer and devotion app made by Becket Marshall in Texas, USA. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. Questions: privacy@pocketchapel.app.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, and the name you choose to show. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address they share with us. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive a private relay address instead.
  • Your answers in the welcome quiz: for example, your church or tradition, and the kinds of prayer you like. These are optional: you can skip the quiz, or choose "View All" instead of a tradition. We use them only to suggest prayers and Bible translations that fit you.
  • Your prayer life in the app: the devotions and stacks you add, devotions and prayers you write yourself, names you add to a prayer list (for example, your children's names), the days you finish them, your streaks and milestones, favorite prayers, memory verses and scores, and personal reflections you write. Personal reflections, your own prayers and your prayer lists are visible only to you (a devotion you create is also visible to a group you share it with).
  • Groups: the groups you create or join, your messages, reactions, comments, prayer intentions, and the reports and blocks you make. People in your group can see your name, your messages and your group progress.
  • Prayer requests: when you ask us to add a prayer, we receive your request together with your name and email, so we can answer you.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, we store your device's notification address. We use it to send your reminders and group updates.
  • Purchases: if you buy Premium, Apple or Google handles the payment. We never see your card. We receive your subscription status through RevenueCat, the service that checks purchases for us.
  • Your time zone: so a prayer day starts at 3 a.m. where you are, and groups count your days correctly.
  • Saved on your phone: your devotions, recent prayers and Bible chapters are kept on your phone so they work without a signal, and sent to us when you're back online. In a group, members can see when you've marked yourself as going offline.
  • Email codes: if you sign up or reset a password with email, we send a one-time code to that address.
  • Anonymous usage counts: for example, which suggested devotion people pick first. These counts aren't tied to your name or email.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or use advertising trackers.

Services that help run the app

  • Supabase stores the app's data and handles sign-in.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in (if you use them), payments and push notifications.
  • Expo delivers push notifications.
  • RevenueCat checks Premium purchases.
  • Resend sends our emails.

Each of these services may only use your information to provide its service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. You can delete your account anytime in the app (Profile → Account → Delete account). This removes your account and the information tied to it. This includes the messages you sent in groups. A devotion you made that a group is still praying stays for that group, without your name. If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also disconnects Pocket Chapel from your Apple ID. Copies in backups are erased within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You can view and change your name, quiz answers and devotions in the app. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings, and you can delete your account. To ask for a copy of your information, or for anything else, email privacy@pocketchapel.app. Depending on where you live, for example in California or the European Union, you may have extra rights: to access, correct, delete or move your data, and to object to how it's used. We honor these requests for everyone.

Children

Pocket Chapel is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from younger children. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Your data travels encrypted, and only you (and, for group content, your group) can read your content in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for big changes, tell you in the app.
